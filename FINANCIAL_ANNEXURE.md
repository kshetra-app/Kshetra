## STREAM 1: Political Advertising (Largest Revenue Driver)

Political parties spend ₹10,000–15,000 crore per general election on advertising. Digital is the fastest-growing slice (~30% and rising).

**What we offer**: Hyper-targeted constituency-level ad placements. A candidate can advertise ONLY in their constituency's feed — no wastage.

| Year | Political Ad Revenue |
|------|---------------------|
| Y1 | ₹20L (local candidates, state elections) |
| Y2 | ₹1.5 Cr (more states, larger parties notice) |
| Y3 | ₹12 Cr (**General election year** — national parties spend heavily) |
| Y4 | ₹4 Cr (off-year, state elections only) |
| Y5 | ₹8 Cr (state elections + pre-election positioning for next cycle) |

**Why realistic**: Even ₹12 Cr in Y3 is just 0.01% of total political ad spend. Facebook/Google take thousands of crores from Indian political ads — we just need a tiny fraction with better targeting.

# Key Insight: Revenue Shifts Over Time

| Phase | Primary Revenue Driver | Why |
|-------|----------------------|-----|
| **Year 1-2** | B2B (Media + Campaigns) | Low user base, but high-value clients pay for data |
| **Year 3** | Political Ads + B2B | Election year = massive ad budgets + every party/media house needs tools |
| **Year 4-5** | B2C (Subscriptions + Consumer Ads) | Large user base unlocked, recurring revenue dominates |

**This means**: Early revenue comes from the media partner (Eenadu/ETV deal) and political parties. Long-term, the consumer base becomes the gold mine.
