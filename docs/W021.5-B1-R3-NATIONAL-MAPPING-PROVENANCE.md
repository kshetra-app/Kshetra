## 1. Executive Summary & Core Objective

The primary objective of **W021.5-B1-R3** is to establish absolute provenance, source-of-truth traceability, and rigorous consistency across the national AC↔PC relationship data plane.

The architecture explicitly differentiates between four fundamental states:
1. **A relationship existing in the database:** Schema capability deployed in PostgreSQL (`public.constituency_parliamentary_mappings`).
2. **A relationship being internally consistent:** Zero unmapped current ACs (4,123 / 4,123), zero duplicate assignments (1 AC = 1 PC), zero inter-state misattributions (AC state == PC state), and temporal range exclusion enforced by PostgreSQL GiST (`uq_cpm_no_temporal_overlap`).
3. **A relationship being reconciled:** Deterministically mapped from official ECI statutory delimitation schedules and boundary crosswalks passing all mathematical seat-count invariants ($N_{\text{AC}} = \sum_{\text{PC}} N_{\text{AC}}^{\text{PC}}$).
4. **A relationship being independently verified against authoritative evidence:** Formally cross-audited against published Gazette statutory schedules and reorganisation acts with line-by-line statutory proof.

A comprehensive static analysis of the generator script (`scripts/generate-migration-061-mappings.mjs`) confirms:

- **Zero Geographic Proximity Inference:** No bounding box or centroid nearest-neighbor logic was applied.
- **Zero Political Occupancy Inference:** No current MP, MLA, candidate name, or party affiliation was consulted.
- **Zero Legacy Seed Lookup:** No dependency on `data/seed/**` or World-A JSON mocks.

All mappings were resolved via **exact normalized statutory name crosswalk** or **whole-state statutory invariant**.

1. **Jammu & Kashmir / Ladakh:**
   - Pre-2019 State structure bifurcated under J&K Reorganisation Act 2019.
   - J&K (90 ACs) delimited under Delimitation Commission Order No. 2 (2022) with effective date `2022-05-20`.
   - Exact mathematical partition: 5 PCs $\times$ 18 ACs each.
   - Ladakh established as separate Union Territory with 1 PC (`LA-PC-01`) and 0 ACs under regime `mha_jk_reorg_2019`.
   - J&K mappings classified honestly as `RECONCILED` against Delimitation Commission Order No. 2 (2022) schedule.
2. **Andhra Pradesh / Telangana:**
   - Pre-2014 unified Andhra Pradesh (294 ACs / 42 PCs) delimited under Delimitation Order 2008.
   - Bifurcated effective `2014-06-02` under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 into residual AP (175 ACs / 25 PCs) and Telangana (119 ACs / 17 PCs).
   - Uniform partition invariant: All 25 AP PCs and 17 TS PCs contain exactly 7 ACs each ($25 \times 7 = 175$, $17 \times 7 = 119$).
   - Both regimes (`eci_delimitation_2008` and `eci_delimitation_2014_ap_ts`) registered in `public.delimitation_regimes`.
3. **Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu (DNH & DD):**
   - Pre-2020 two distinct UTs: Dadra & Nagar Haveli (`DN-PC-01`) and Daman & Diu (`DD-PC-01`), both with 0 ACs.
   - Merged under the Merger of Union Territories Act, 2019 effective `2020-01-26`.
   - Post-merger UT (`DN`) catalogued with 0 ACs and 2 PCs under regime `eci_delimitation_2019_dnh_dd`.
4. **Assam 2023:**
   - Delimitation Order 2008 preserved under Section 8A of Representation of the People Act 1950.
   - ECI Final Order No. 282/AS/2023 (August 11, 2023) reorganized internal boundaries while retaining 126 ACs / 14 PCs.
   - Canonical versioning in `public.constituency_lineage` is prepared to store the 2023 version transition when geospatial polygon geometries are ingested.
