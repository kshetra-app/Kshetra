## 4. CRITICAL FORENSIC FINDINGS & DATA DEFECTS

### A. Compound Runner-Up Strings (836 instances)
In `data/seed/chhattisgarh-constituencies.ts`, `haryana-constituencies.ts`, `rajasthan-constituencies.ts`, and `jharkhand-constituencies.ts`, runner-up data was concatenated into strings like `'INC - Gulab Kamro'`, `'BJP - Hem Singh Bhadana'`, and `'AAZADSAM - Rohitash Kumar'`.
* **Resolution Protocol:** The ingestion engine must apply a regex split on `\s+-\s+` to separate the political party from the human candidate name.

## 5. POLITICAL ENTITY LINEAGE INVARIANTS

### A. Strict Separation of Split Factions
1. **Shiv Sena:**
   * `ORG-PARTY-SHS`: Shiv Sena (Eknath Shinde faction, awarded Bow & Arrow symbol and original name by ECI).
   * `ORG-PARTY-SHSUBT`: Shiv Sena (Uddhav Balasaheb Thackeray, Flaming Torch symbol).
   * These are legally distinct entities in ECI records and must never be merged.
2. **Nationalist Congress Party:**
   * `ORG-PARTY-NCP`: Nationalist Congress Party (Ajit Pawar faction, Clock symbol).
   * `ORG-PARTY-NCPSP`: Nationalist Congress Party (Sharadchandra Pawar, Man Blowing Turha symbol).
   * Legally distinct entities.

### B. Successor Organizations & Renamings
* **TRS $\to$ BRS:** Telangana Rashtra Samithi officially changed its name to Bharat Rashtra Samithi on October 5, 2022 (ECI Notification 56/2022).
  * Modeled in `public.organization_relationships` as `relationship_type = 'renamed_to'`.
  * Pre-2022 tenures point to `ORG-PARTY-TRS`; post-2022 tenures point to `ORG-PARTY-BRS`.

### C. Mergers
* `ORG-PARTY-LJD` (Loktantrik Janata Dal) merged into `ORG-PARTY-RJD` in March 2022.
* `ORG-PARTY-PDF` (People's Democratic Front, Meghalaya) merged into `ORG-PARTY-NPP` in May 2023.

### D. Alliances vs. Parties
* Alliances (`ORG-ALLIANCE-NDA`, etc.) have `org_type = 'political_alliance'`.
* Parties belong to alliances via `public.organization_relationships` (`alliance_with` or `coalition_partner`).
* Candidates and politicians **never** belong to alliances; they belong to political parties or are independent.

### E. Independent Candidates
* `IND`, `Independent`, `INDP` represent candidate status, not an organization.
* **Creating a synthetic party entity (`ORG-INDEPENDENT` or `ORG-PARTY-IND`) is strictly forbidden.**
* Modeled exclusively on `candidacies.is_independent = true`.
