## 2. Part A — Resolution of the 4,120 vs. 4,123 Source Coverage Discrepancy

### A1. The Source Universe Discrepancy

The historical source `scripts/india_2012-17_AC.csv` (derived from ECI 2008 Delimitation national tables) reports **4,120 distinct Assembly Constituencies**. Conversely, the canonical national registry established in Migration 059 contains **4,123 Assembly Constituencies**.

Through programmatic inspection of all statutory schedules, the discrepancy is resolved as follows:
- **Non-Jammu & Kashmir Canonical Baseline:** 27 States + 3 UTs (Delhi, Puducherry) account for exactly **4,033 Assembly Constituencies**.
- **Pre-2019 / 2008 Delimitation J&K Baseline:** Under the Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir and the 2008 Delimitation Order, Jammu & Kashmir possessed **87 statutory Assembly Constituencies**.
  $$\text{Pre-2019 Total} = 4,033 + 87 = \mathbf{4,120 \text{ ACs}}$$
- **2022 Delimitation Commission Reconstitution:** Following the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, the Delimitation Commission issued **Final Order No. 2, 2022** (notified under MHA Gazette S.O. 2223(E), effective 2022-05-20), reconstituting the Jammu & Kashmir Legislative Assembly to **90 statutory seats** (+3 net seats: 43 in Jammu division, 47 in Kashmir division).
  $$\text{Post-2022 Statutory Canonical Universe} = 4,033 + 90 = \mathbf{4,123 \text{ ACs}}$$

## 4. Part C & D — Constituency Version vs. Lineage Semantics

### Architectural Definitions
1. **Constituency (`public.constituencies`):** Represents the persistent constitutional electoral seat identity (UUID). It remains invariant across delimitation cycles.
2. **Constituency Version (`public.constituency_versions`):** Represents the statutory territorial geometry and reservation profile of that seat during a specific delimitation regime and time interval $[valid\_from, valid\_to)$.
3. **Constituency Lineage (`public.constituency_lineage`):** A directed acyclic graph (DAG) connecting a predecessor version (`source_constituency_version_id`) to a successor version (`target_constituency_version_id`) when boundaries are redrawn, merged, split, or renamed across delimitation regimes.

### Why Zero Lineage Rows is Semantically Correct at Milestone B1
In Milestone W021.5-B1 (Migration 059), only the **single current baseline version** of each constituency was ingested (exactly 1 version record per AC).

Constituency lineage is a relationship between **two distinct version records**. Because predecessor versions (e.g. 1976 Delimitation Order versions or pre-2019 J&K state versions) have not yet been backfilled into `public.constituency_versions`, inserting rows into `public.constituency_lineage` would require referencing non-existent UUIDs. This would immediately violate the foreign key constraints:
```sql
source_constituency_version_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES public.constituency_versions(id) ON DELETE RESTRICT
target_constituency_version_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES public.constituency_versions(id) ON DELETE RESTRICT
```

Fabricating dummy lineage rows would corrupt referential integrity. Therefore, **0 rows in `public.constituency_lineage` is the only semantically truthful and relational state** at Milestone B1.

## 6. Part F — Database Schema Safeguards for Lineage

In `supabase/migrations/060_canonical_electoral_geography_remediation.sql`, the lineage layer is hardened with:
- **Foreign Key Restraints:** Both `source_constituency_version_id` and `target_constituency_version_id` enforce `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.
- **Self-Link Prevention Constraint:** Added `CONSTRAINT chk_constituency_lineage_no_self_link CHECK (source_constituency_version_id <> target_constituency_version_id)`.
- **Composite Unique Constraint:** `CONSTRAINT uq_constituency_lineage UNIQUE (source_constituency_version_id, target_constituency_version_id, relationship_type, effective_date)`.
- **Controlled Enum Constraint:** `relationship_type` is restricted strictly to: `CONTINUES_AS`, `RENAMED_AS`, `RENUMBERED_AS`, `REPLACED_BY`, `SPLIT_INTO`, `MERGED_INTO`, `ABOLISHED`.
