## 3. Statutory Exceptions & Special Regime Partitions

### 3.1 Jammu & Kashmir Delimitation Order No. 2 (2022)
- Reorganized following the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019.
- Delimitation Commission Order No. 2 (effective 2022-05-20) structured the UT into **90 Assembly Constituencies** and **5 Parliamentary Constituencies**.
- Invariant: Each PC in J&K contains **exactly 18 Assembly Constituencies**:
  - `JK-PC-01` (Anantnag-Rajouri): ACs 37–47, 84–90 (combines Anantnag, Kulgam, Shopian, Rajouri, and Poonch)
  - `JK-PC-02` (Baramulla): ACs 1–16, 27–28 (Kupwara, Baramulla, Bandipora, Budgam)
  - `JK-PC-03` (Jammu): ACs 56–58, 69–83 (Jammu, Samba, Reasi, and Kalakote-Sunderbani)
  - `JK-PC-04` (Srinagar): ACs 17–26, 29–36 (Srinagar, Ganderbal, Budgam, Pulwama, Shopian)
  - `JK-PC-05` (Udhampur): ACs 48–55, 59–68 (Udhampur, Kathua, Doda, Kishtwar, Ramban)
- Ladakh was created as a separate UT with sole PC `LA-PC-01` and zero assembly constituencies.

### 3.2 Andhra Pradesh & Telangana (AP Reorganisation Act, 2014)
- Delimitation Order 2008 Schedule XXXI initially covered composite Andhra Pradesh (42 PCs, 294 ACs).
- AP Reorganisation Act 2014 partitioned the territory effective 2014-06-02:
  - **Telangana:** 17 PCs (`TS-PC-01` to `TS-PC-17`), 119 ACs (exactly 7 ACs per PC: $17 \times 7 = 119$).
  - **Residuary Andhra Pradesh:** 25 PCs (`AP-PC-01` to `AP-PC-25`), 175 ACs (exactly 7 ACs per PC: $25 \times 7 = 175$).
- Reconciled statutory naming:
  - `AP-AC-041 Kakinada City` -> `AP-PC-11` (Kakinada)
  - `AP-AC-151 Tadipatri` -> `AP-PC-03` (Ananthapur)
  - `AP-AC-169 Sathyavedu` -> `AP-PC-22` (Thirupathi)

### 3.3 Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu (Merger Act, 2020)
- The Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (Merger of Union Territories) Act, 2019 unified the territories into single UT `DH` effective 2020-01-26.
- Retains 2 Parliamentary Constituencies:
  - `DH-PC-01`: Dadra and Nagar Haveli
  - `DH-PC-02`: Daman and Diu
- Contains zero Assembly Constituencies.

### 3.4 Sikkim Sangha Assembly Constituency (`SK-AC-032`)
- Article 371F of the Constitution of India provides for the Sangha constituency, a non-territorial monastic seat representing registered Buddhist monks of Sikkim's monasteries.
- Statutorily mapped to Sikkim's sole statewide Parliamentary Constituency `SK-PC-01`.

### 3.5 Assam Delimitation Order (2023)
- ECI notified final delimitation for Assam on 2023-08-11: 126 ACs and 14 PCs.

## 4. Database-Enforced Temporal Integrity Architecture

### 4.1 Chronological Check Constraint (`chk_cpm_dates`)
```sql
ALTER TABLE public.constituency_parliamentary_mappings
ADD CONSTRAINT chk_cpm_dates CHECK (effective_to IS NULL OR effective_from < effective_to);
```
- Guarantees valid directional intervals (`effective_from < effective_to`).
- Rejects zero-length intervals (`effective_from == effective_to`).
- Rejects inverted intervals (`effective_to < effective_from`).

### 4.2 Temporal Exclusion Constraint (`uq_cpm_no_temporal_overlap`)
```sql
ALTER TABLE public.constituency_parliamentary_mappings
ADD CONSTRAINT uq_cpm_no_temporal_overlap EXCLUDE USING gist (
  assembly_constituency_version_id WITH =,
  (daterange(effective_from, effective_to, '[)')) WITH &&
);
```
- Leverages PostgreSQL `btree_gist` index extension.
- Half-open interval `[effective_from, effective_to)` ensures that contiguous transitions:
  `[2008-02-19, 2026-01-01)` and `[2026-01-01, NULL)` are **permitted without collision**.
- Overlapping intervals for the same AC version are **strictly rejected with error 23P01 (exclusion_violation)**.

### 4.3 Single-Current Mapping Partial Unique Index (`uq_cpm_single_current_ac`)
```sql
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS uq_cpm_single_current_ac
ON public.constituency_parliamentary_mappings(assembly_constituency_version_id)
WHERE is_current = true;
```
- Guarantees at the storage engine level that no active AC version can ever point to more than one current Parliamentary Constituency.
