## 1. Executive Summary & Remediation Objectives

Milestone W021.5-B2.2-B remediates all four foundational political organization architectural and schema deficiencies (`GAP-ORG-001` through `GAP-ORG-004`) identified during the forensic audit in B2.2-A. Prior to this milestone, the political organization database layer lacked multilingual representations across Indian scripts, did not maintain an indexed organization alias registry, could not represent temporal transitions of statutory election symbols, and permitted synthetic independent identities or classification of independent status as a recognition level.

Through Migration `064_political_organization_governance_remediation.sql`, coordinated TypeScript contracts in `@kshetra/shared`, and Reconciliation Ledger V2, B2.2-B establishes a deterministic, provenance-backed organization governance plane. All 1,096 raw party strings from the national repository are categorized under strict confidence metrics (`VERIFIED`, `RECONCILED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `CONFLICTING`, `MISSING`), all 37 single-letter MP records and 13 truncated MLA records are forensic-audited, independent candidate boundaries are strictly enforced without synthetic party records, and unresolved placeholders (`SHORTNAM - Baljeet Yadav`) are held provisional pending Form 21E historical gazette audit.

## 2. Core Architectural & Schema Remediation Matrix

| Gap ID | Identified Deficiency | Remediated Database Object | Governance / Schema Mechanism |

| **GAP-ORG-002** | Absence of deterministic alias registry | `public.organization_aliases` | `raw_lookup_key`, `raw_original_string`, temporal bounds (`valid_from`, `valid_to`), partial unique indexes for national and state scopes; RLS enabled. |
| **GAP-ORG-003** | Lack of temporal symbol tracking | `public.organization_symbols` | Tracks symbol name, asset URL, jurisdiction scope, temporal validity (`valid_from`, `valid_to`), current status (`is_current`), statutory order ref, and `uq_org_symbols_timeline` unique constraint; RLS enabled. |

## 5. Independent Candidate Boundary Governance

* **Database Rule:** Independent candidates are strictly represented via `candidacy.is_independent = true` and `candidacy.organization_id = NULL`.
* **Zero Synthetic Organizations:** The constraint `chk_prohibit_synthetic_independent` prohibits any entity in `public.political_organizations` with IDs matching `ORG-INDEPENDENT`, `ORG-PARTY-IND`, `ORG-PARTY-INDP`, or `ORG-PARTY-INDEPENDENT`.
* **Prohibited Recognition Level:** The check constraint `political_organizations_recognition_level_check` allows only `'national'`, `'state'`, `'unrecognized'`, and `'registered_unrecognized'`. `'independent'` is eliminated from all organization schemas.
