## 2. Statutory Timelines & Grievance Redressal Mechanism

1. **Acknowledgment**: Every complaint received through the web intake form (`/policy/grievance`), email, or registered post is issued a unique tracking ticket within **24 hours**.
2. **Investigation & Disposal**: Complaints are reviewed by the Trust & Safety team and disposed of with a reasoned order within **15 days** from receipt.
3. **Emergency Expedited Takedowns**:
   - Content violating Rule 3(2)(b) (non-consensual sexually explicit content, deepfakes, impersonation) is acted upon within **24 hours** of receipt.
   - Violations of Section 69A of the Information Technology Act (court orders or government blocking orders) are executed within the required statutory window.
