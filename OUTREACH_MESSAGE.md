## Key Principles in All Messages:

1. **Respectful Telugu etiquette** — "garu", "namaskaramulu"
2. **State the value in one line** — Don't make them read a paragraph to understand what you want
3. **Small ask** — "15 minutes" is non-threatening. No one says no to 15 minutes.
4. **Show, don't tell** — Offer a demo, not a pitch deck
5. **No desperation** — You're offering something valuable, not begging
6. **Easy exit** — "If it doesn't interest you, I'll thank you and leave" removes pressure
