> **Assessment:** The current duplicate-ledger rule of retaining the *first* natural-key occurrence **does NOT reliably preserve the most complete and authoritative political information**.

### Rationale
1. **Positional Bias:** Retaining the first occurrence assumes that upstream input files are sorted in descending order of data quality. In reality, `mla-profiles.ts` contained rich biographical data (dob, education, criminal records) but had a fragile party parser, whereas `constituencies.ts` had authoritative party and election outcome data.
2. **Loss of Attribution:** By dropping the second occurrence unconditionally via `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, the database loses the canonical `party_id` and the actual `votes_received` for all 36 candidates.
3. **Pipeline Lesson:** For future ingestions, deduplication must use **completeness-aware consolidation (field-level COALESCE / enrichment merge)** rather than naive positional dropping.
