In strict compliance with **CTO Directive 128**, this report establishes the substantive closure of evidence claims, source-resolution verification, locator concordance, and truthful reporting for the 36 curated postal-to-assembly constituency records in Kshetra.

Gate recommendation dynamically evaluates to rejection if any check fails or unwarranted claims are asserted.
6. **Workstream F — Substantive Negative Test Battery (26 Distinct Scenarios):** Expanded negative test suite from 14 to 26 distinct scenarios (14 structural + 12 substantive scenarios). - Generic locators that merely name `Schedule XXXI, Table B` or `Schedule II, Table B` without entry or ward coordinates are strictly **REJECTED** (Scenario 11).

  - Locators must specify the exact Entry Number (e.g., `Schedule XXXI, Table B, Entry 66`).
  - The entry number in the locator must match `assignedAcNumber` (Scenario 17).
  - The cited entry must exist in Delimitation Order 2008 extracts (Scenario 16).

  - Locators must detail the composite intersection or multiple ward distribution (e.g., GHMC Wards 78, 10/11, 3 parts).
  - Candidate AC numbers must be concordant with candidate entries in the schedule.

## 4. Workstream C — Genuine Source-Claim Verification & Nine Orthogonal Dimensions

In accordance with Section 1 and Section 4 of Directive 128, evidence claims are decoupled across nine distinct dimensions:

| Dimension | Scope & Rule | Evaluated In | Status / Outcome Across 36 Records |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Document Field Completeness** | All 15 required metadata fields are non-empty strings | CHECK 5 | 36/36 Non-empty |
| **2. Source Document Resolution** | Reference document resolves to in-repo dossiers or explicitly flagged external gazette | CHECK 6 | 36/36 Resolved (In-repo dossier resolved, external gazette noted) |
| **3. Exact Locator Resolution** | Schedule, table, entry number, and ward coordinates verified | CHECK 7 | 36/36 Exact locators verified |
| **4. Source-Passage Content** | Delimitation Order 2008 statutory text extract inspected | CHECK 7, 8 | 36/36 Extracts inspected |
| **5. Claim Support Status** | Relationship between claim, passage, and conclusion | CHECK 8 | 36/36 `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` |
| **6. Source Authenticity** | In-repo dossier extract vs official statutory publication | CHECK 6, 8 | In-repo extract inspected; external gazette unverified |
| **7. Constituency Identity** | Number, name, and administrative composition verified | CHECK 4, 7, 8 | 36/36 Authoritatively verified |
| **8. Postal-to-Constituency Linkage** | Postal delivery beat coverage relative to constituency | CHECK 3, 8 | 32 Provisional, 4 Ambiguous (Fail-closed) |
| **9. Geographic Containment** | Spatial polygon containment of postal delivery beat | CHECK 3, 8 | 36/36 Unverified (`spatialContainmentVerified: false`) |

### Claim Taxonomy Classification
- All 36 entries are classified as `claimSupportStatus === 'PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED'`:
  - The inspected source passage **substantively supports** the statutory constituency identity, number, name, and municipal ward composition.
  - The inspected source passage **does NOT prove** postal delivery beat spatial containment, because Delimitation Order 2008 defines administrative units, not India Post delivery beats.
- Any record asserting `spatialContainmentVerified === true` on administrative evidence is **REJECTED** (Scenario 19).
- Any record asserting `claimSupportStatus === 'UNSUPPORTED_BY_INSPECTED_SOURCE'` is **REJECTED** from the curated set (Scenario 20).
