## 2. Corrective Action A: Honest Evidence Independence

### 2.1 The Critical Distinction: Structural Separation vs. Geographic Proof
The builder script (`scripts/build_pin_independent_evidence_manifest.mjs`) authors `data/evidence/w021_5/pin_independent_evidence_manifest.json` independently of the runtime code. The validator (`scripts/verify_pin_evidence_matrix.cjs` and `.mjs`) audits `CURATED_PROVISIONAL_PIN_DIRECTORY` against this manifest. 

Under Directive 126, the evidentiary scope is honestly defined:
- **What this proves:** Structural separation between evidence documentation and application code. The application cannot silently drift from recorded evidence without failing automated tests.
- **What this does NOT prove:** It does **NOT** prove statutory geographic containment of postal delivery beats. In Indian electoral law, ECI Delimitation Orders (2008) define assembly constituencies strictly by **administrative territorial units** (Municipal Corporation Wards, Municipalities, Mandals, and Revenue Villages). India Post PIN codes are operational logistics routing beats created without statutory alignment to assembly constituency boundaries.
- **The 4-Tier Confidence Taxonomy:**
  - `AUTHORITATIVELY_VERIFIED: 0` (Zero PIN codes possess statutory 1:1 delimitation orders or verified voter-roll GIS polygons).
  - `PROVISIONALLY_APPROXIMATED: 32` (Plausible single-AC approximations backed by municipal ward concordance or post office locality centroids).
  - `AMBIGUOUS_MULTI_CONSTITUENCY: 4` (`500001`, `500028`, `500034`, `500056` fail-closed with `canonicalAcNo: undefined` and documented candidate sets).
  - `UNVERIFIED_OR_EXTERNALLY_BLOCKED: 0` maintained (Unmapped PINs fail-closed at runtime with 404 PIN_NOT_FOUND).

### 2.2 Enrichment of All 36 Records
Every record in `pin_independent_evidence_manifest.json` now carries 15 explicit documentary fields:
1. `sourceInspectionStatus`: `'IN_REPO_DOSSIER_INSPECTED'` (ECI Delimitation Order 2008 Schedule XXXI extract checked in at `data/evidence/w015_b2/eci_delimitation_2008_schedule_xxxi.txt` and Schedule II dossier at `data/evidence/w020/authoritative/eci_delimitation_order_2008_source_dossier.md`).
2. `statutorySourceStatus`: `'EXTERNAL_STATUTORY_PUBLICATION_NOT_CHECKED_IN'` (Full official gazette scans are external publications not stored in git).
3. `propositionSupported`: `'CONSTITUENCY_IDENTITY_AND_MUNICIPAL_WARD_COMPOSITION'` (for single AC) or `'MULTI_CONSTITUENCY_WARD_INTERSECTION'` (for multi-AC).
4. `directEvidenceVsInference`: Explicit statement distinguishing statutory ward extent from postal delivery centroid approximation.
5. `unsupportedOrInaccessibleClaims`: Plain disclosure that statutory 6-digit postal delivery beat GIS polygons are unpublished by India Post and inaccessible.
