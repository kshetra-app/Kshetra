### 3.2 Baseline Comparison Role vs Independent Authority Anchor

Under Rule IV-001, this delivery establishes the critical architectural distinction between internal historical comparison and independent external authority:
- **Baseline Comparison Role:** Commit `be4ba41f` is recorded in `scripts/reconcile_execution_ledger.mjs` as `CANONICAL_HISTORICAL_ORIGIN_COMMIT`. Its sole role is an immutable internal comparison baseline to detect post-baseline drift and prevent unauthorized additions (such as phantom task `B1-05`) or unapproved weight compressions. It does NOT represent external independent approval authority.
- **Proposed External Authority Architecture (`PROPOSED_NOT_ACTIVE`):** Independent authority requires an out-of-band cryptographic anchor outside the repository's commit DAG (e.g. Cosign keyless signatures via OIDC or GPG signed root manifest).
