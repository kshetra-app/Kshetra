## 3. Workstream B — Close the 36-PIN Evidence Gap

### 3.1 Domain Rule: Postal Beat vs. Assembly Constituency Delimitation
A fundamental finding established under Directive 125 is the legal and geographic separation between postal delivery beats and statutory assembly constituencies:
1. **ECI Delimitation Orders (2008):** Under Article 82 and Article 170 of the Constitution of India, the Delimitation Commission defines assembly constituencies in terms of statutory administrative units: Municipal Corporations, Municipal Wards, Mandals, and Revenue Villages.
2. **Department of Posts (DoP) PIN Codes:** A PIN code is an operational **mail delivery logistics routing beat** managed by the Department of Posts (Ministry of Communications). It does **not** constitute an administrative or electoral boundary.
3. **Absence of Statutory 1:1 Overlays:** In India, no statute, gazette order, or official crosswalk establishes that a 6-digit postal code is coterminous with an assembly constituency. Postal beats routinely cross municipal ward, mandal, and constituency perimeters.
4. **Taxonomy Enforcement:**
   - **Authoritatively Verified (`0`):** Zero PIN codes in India can be claimed as authoritatively verified 1:1 to an assembly constituency without an official statutory GIS polygon overlay of the postal delivery beat or an ECI polling-station crosswalk.
   - **Provisionally Approximated (`32`):** Single-AC mappings are provisional approximations supported by municipal ward concordance or postal head office centroids.
   - **Ambiguous Multi-Constituency (`4`):** Dense urban delivery zones spanning &ge;2 constituencies (`500001`, `500028`, `500034`, `500056`) fail closed with `canonicalAcNo: undefined` and documented candidate sets.
   - **Unverified / Externally Blocked (`0` maintained):** Unmapped PINs fail closed at runtime with `404 PIN_NOT_FOUND`.
