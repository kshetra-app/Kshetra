## 6. Root-Cause Analysis Across Pipelines

1. **Extraction Source Naivety:** Builders scraped secondary websites (Wikipedia and MyNeta) using ad-hoc scripts that lacked validation against statutory ECI Form 20/21E returns.
2. **Textual AST Extraction without Context:** In `scripts/generate-b2-2e-forensic-manifests.mjs`, regex brace-matching scanned entire `.ts` files, extracting internal helper arrays (`ENRICHED_PROFILES`) and treating them as primary candidate filings.
3. **Absence of Temporal Dimensions:** Current schemas and seed structures represent the current representative as a scalar property (`currentMLA: string`). They do not model election events, tenures, by-elections, or vacancies with effective start and end dates.
4. **Client Adapter Temporal Inversion:** `apps/mobile/lib/stateDataAdapter.ts` mapped `winner2026` as current reality for 2021 election states, allowing simulated projections to override real sitting members.
