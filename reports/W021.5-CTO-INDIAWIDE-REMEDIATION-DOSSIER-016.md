### 1.2 Summary of Independent Adjudication Findings
1. **Local Gangavathi Corrections (AC 61) are Validated:**
   The corrections made to `data/seed/karnataka-constituencies.ts` and `data/seed/karnataka-mla-profiles.ts` accurately reflect the official 2023 Karnataka Legislative Assembly election return (ECI Form 20/21E): G. Janardhan Reddy (KRPP) won Gangavathi with 66,213 votes against Iqbal Ansari (INC) with 57,947 votes (margin: 8,266 votes).
2. **Bellary Rural AC 76 Reassignment is Factually False:**
   Directive 015 reassigned `Bellary Rural` from `acNo: 61` to `acNo: 76` in `data/seed/karnataka-historical-results.ts` purely as a synthetic uniqueness hack to satisfy `new Set(acNos).size === 224`. In statutory reality (ECI Delimitation Order 2008 and Migration 059 canonical registry), Bellary Rural is **KA-AC-65**, while AC 76 is **Shorapur**. In fact, `karnataka-historical-results.ts` has **194 AC number mismatches** out of 224 seats because it was copied from a sequential Wikipedia district list.
3. **Severe Systematic Seed Anomalies in Karnataka:**
   Inspection of `data/seed/karnataka-constituencies.ts` revealed **15 synthetic candidates** with `" Jr"` appended (e.g., `Umesh Katti Jr`, `H D Deve Gowda Jr`, `Siddaramaiah Jr`, `B Sriramulu Jr`) and **5 fabricated, non-existent assembly constituencies** (`Jevargi South`, `Chikkaballapura South`, `Gauribidanur South`, `Channasandra`, `Bangalore Rural`).
4. **Mobile 2026 Projections Leakage Confirmed:**
   Directive 015 claimed the mobile adapter was decoupled from future projections. Independent code audit reveals this claim was false: `apps/mobile/lib/stateDataAdapter.ts` actively maps `winnerParty: c.winner2026` for Tamil Nadu (234 seats), Kerala (140 seats), West Bengal (294 seats), and passes 2026 into `genericAdapter` for Assam (126 seats) and Puducherry (30 seats). Real mobile screens present unheld 2026 simulated election projections as current winners.
5. **Test Invariant Quality Audit:**
   Of the 14 invariant tests in `tests/political-truth-audit.test.mjs`, only 7 test actual repository files. The remaining 7 (including Invariant 06 for future projections) tested local mock functions and fixtures defined inside the test itself. If the mobile app serves simulated 2026 winners to end-users, Invariant 06 still passes.
6. **Nationwide Verification Truth:**
   Only **240 out of 4,123 assembly constituencies (5.8%)** have been deeply compared with statutory ECI Form 20/21E returns. **3,883 constituencies (94.2%) remain unverified** against primary returns. The claim in Directive 015 that 3,711 were unverified resulted from an invalid subtraction of defective records from the total (`4,123 - 412 = 3,711`).

## 4. Historical AC-Number Forensic Audit

### 4.1 Forensic Analysis of the Karnataka 2018 AC 61 / AC 76 Collision
In Directive 015, line 95 (`Bellary Rural`) was modified from `acNo: 61` to `acNo: 76`.
Our independent audit examined the statutory ECI Delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies Order (2008) and Kshetra's canonical database registry in `supabase/migrations/059_canonical_national_constituency_registry.sql`:

```

│ Gangavathi                      │ 61       │ KA-AC-061                  │ 61 (remediated)        │
│ Bellary City                    │ 64       │ KA-AC-064                  │ 60 (SHIFTED -4)        │
│ Bellary Rural                   │ 65       │ KA-AC-065                  │ 76 (ARBITRARY HACK)    │

└─────────────────────────────────┴──────────┴────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┘
```

### 4.2 Systematic Findings in `data/seed/karnataka-historical-results.ts`
1. **The Bellary Rural Reassignment to AC 76 is Factually False:**
   Bellary Rural was never AC 76 in any delimitation order. AC 76 is Shorapur. Reassigning Bellary Rural to 76 was a synthetic hack that resolved duplicate detection while introducing a false constituency identifier.
2. **194 Historical AC Number Shifts:**
   The entire 2018 Karnataka historical seed was generated from an unnumbered Wikipedia district overview table, with sequential numbers `1` through `224` applied to district groupings. Because Wikipedia grouped Belgaum, Bagalkot, and Vijayapura districts differently than the official clockwise ECI delimitation schedule, **194 out of 224 constituencies carry false AC numbers**.
3. **Triplication of Shorapur:**
   Shorapur appears three times in the historical seed:
   - Line 133: `{ acNo: 94, name: 'Shorapur', winner: 'Nagangouda Kandkur', party: 'INC' }`
   - Line 136: `{ acNo: 97, name: 'Surpur', winner: 'Raju Gowda', party: 'INC' }`
   - Line 276: `{ acNo: 223, name: 'Shorapur', winner: 'Raja Venkatappa Nayak', party: 'INC' }`
4. **Adjudication Decision:**
   `DEFECT CONFIRMED, FIX PENDING (DEF-004 / DEF-008)`. The current `acNo: 76` in `data/seed/karnataka-historical-results.ts` is quarantined as an unresolved defect. Full re-alignment to Migration 059 canonical identifiers is required before historical lookups can be exposed to consumers.
