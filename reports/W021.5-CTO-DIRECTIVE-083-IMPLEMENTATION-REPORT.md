## 1. Executive Summary & Mission Fulfillment

CTO Directive 083 halts repetitive, isolated checksum-manifest refinement cycles and accelerates core product delivery. Under this directive, Kshetra delivers a working, tested, end-to-end political intelligence experience by integrating the mobile application, Fastify API, shared domain types, and canonical data foundations across all 31 supported states and Union Territories.

The integrated product journey fulfills the required connected flow:
```
Discover a constituency 
  → inspect its political and electoral intelligence 
  → open a representative or candidate profile 
  → examine election history and affidavit disclosures 
  → understand the provenance and completeness of information.
```

All implementations maintain strict adherence to non-negotiable boundaries: zero database migrations, zero SQL mutations, immutable preservation of the 4,523-row affidavit population, strict non-coercion of financial disclosures (never turning unverified/null fields into zeros or manufacturing numbers), preservation of the 17 unverified Delhi assembly records (`DL-AC-071` to `DL-AC-087`), and keeping defect `DQ-W021.5-B2.2E-001` strictly open under Rule IV-001.

## 2. Connected Product Journey Implementation

### 2.1 Section A: Constituency Discovery & Canonical Identity
- **Multi-State National Architecture:** Updated `apps/mobile/lib/globalSearch.ts` to replace the hardcoded 4-state barrier (`TS`, `AP`, `KA`, `MH`) with `getAvailableStates()` from `stateDataAdapter.ts`, instantly expanding discovery across all 31 Indian states and Union Territories.
- **Canonical Identifier Scoring:** Enhanced search matching so exact canonical constituency identifiers (e.g. `TS-AC-65`, `AP-AC-25`, `KA-AC-173`, `MH-AC-38`) receive top score (`score: 100`) and map directly to `/constituency/${c.id}` routes.
- **Direct Representative Navigation:** MLA search results now provide direct routes to legislator profiles (`/legislator/MLA_${stateCode}_${electionYear}_${name}_${acNo}`).
- **Statutory Identity Alignment:** Reconciled constituency numbering and candidate identities between Delimitation data and seed files, supporting both canonical constituency names and statutory AC numbers (e.g. Kodangal AC#72 vs historical seed AC#65).

### 2.2 Section B: Political & Electoral Intelligence
- **Fastify API Routes Hardening (`apps/api/src/routes/constituencies.ts`):**
  - Parameter Resolution: Handles canonical IDs (`TS-AC-65`), bare AC numbers (`65`), and URL-encoded constituency names without 404ing.
  - Eliminated Manufactured Numbers: Purged mock fallback figures (`50000` votes, `'INC'`, `5000` margin) in favor of genuine raw seed values across election years (2025, 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021) or truthful empty/zero values.
  - Multi-State Search: Removed the artificial `if (stateCode !== 'TS')` single-state barrier in `/states/:stateCode/constituencies/search`, enabling multi-state constituency search via the backend service.
- **Mobile API Client Endpoints (`apps/mobile/lib/api/endpoints/constituencies.ts` & `elections.ts`):**
  - Implemented typed `ConstituenciesEndpoint` (`listConstituencies`, `getConstituency`, `searchConstituencies`) and `ElectionsEndpoint` (`listElections`, `getElection`, `listContests`, `getContestDetail`, `getPersonElections`).
  - Integrated runtime schema validators (`validateConstituencyBrief`, `validateConstituencyDetail`, `validateConstituenciesListResponse`) enforcing contract conformity and rejecting malformed payloads.
  - Wired both endpoints onto `ApiClient` in `apps/mobile/lib/api/client.ts` and re-exported through `apps/mobile/lib/api/index.ts`.

### 2.3 Section C: Representative, Candidate & Affidavit Experience
- **Legislator Detail Screen (`apps/mobile/app/legislator/[id].tsx`):**
  - ID Parser: Resolves canonical identifiers (`TS-AC-65`), legislator slugs (`MLA_TS_2023_Kodangal_72`), and legacy IDs.
  - Property Alignment: Resolved critical property mismatch where `_financialRecords` previously omitted required `FinancialRecord` properties. Now correctly passes `electionYear`, `selfMovableAssets`, `selfImmovableAssets`, `spouseMovableAssets`, `spouseImmovableAssets`, `totalAssets`, `totalLiabilities`, `netWorth`, `selfIncome`, and `spouseIncome`.
  - Non-Coercion Transparency: Added an explicit user notice when candidate affidavits are unverified or pending, preventing misleading presentations.
- **Affidavit Presentation (`apps/mobile/components/AffidavitCard.tsx`):**
  - Replaced silent `if (!affidavit) return null;` dropoff with a truthful, non-blocking "Affidavit Disclosure Pending" notice card explaining that official disclosures are being indexed.
- **Financial Breakdown Logic (`apps/mobile/components/legislator/FinancialBreakdownCard.tsx`):**
  - Hardened `resolveBreakdownState` to prioritize `isZeroTotal` status notice ("Affirmative zero assets disclosed") when `totalAssets === 0`, preventing genuine zeros from being treated as missing or corrupted.
- **Affidavit Store (`apps/mobile/stores/affidavits.ts`):**
  - Hardened `getWinnerAffidavit` to match on both `acNo` and canonical `constituencyName` to ensure winner affidavits are located reliably across Delimitation AC shifts.

### 2.4 Section D: Stale-Response Prevention on Rapid Navigation
- **Asynchronous Guard (`apps/mobile/lib/staleResponseGuard.ts`):**
  - Implemented `SequenceGuard` and `RequestToken` ensuring that when users rapidly switch between constituencies or candidate profiles, older in-flight requests that resolve out of order are cleanly ignored and cannot overwrite fresh profile states.
