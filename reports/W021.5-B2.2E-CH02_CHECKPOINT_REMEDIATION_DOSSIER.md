## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & DEFECT REMEDIATION LOG

### 1.1 Identified Defect in Previous Checkpoint
1. **Sampling Deficiency:** The initial draft checkpoint verified only a sparse sample of 5 boundary/sample IDs (`0001`, `0100`, `0500`, `1000`, `4522`) and relied heavily on the overall table count (`count(*) = 1196`).
2. **Non-Contiguous UUID Sequence:** In the B2.2-E dataset, affidavits were partitioned by **State**, not by a contiguous linear range of serial numbers. Candidates from national Parliamentary elections (Lok Sabha) from the respective states carry IDs from `...00004432` up to `...00004522`. A simple scalar min/max boundary check failed to ensure that the exact 1,196 assigned records were present.
3. **Ambiguous Pre- vs Post-Execution Delta:** The previous checkpoint did not isolate the pre-execution baseline from the post-execution delta, risking false attribution if unexpected records existed in the table.

### 1.2 Remediation Implemented
1. **Exact 1,196 ID Set Enumeration:** The corrected checkpoint incorporates an exhaustive CTE (`expected_chunk_02_ids`) defining the exact set of all 1,196 UUIDs assigned to Chunk 02.
2. **Bitwise Set-Difference Assertions:**
   - `chunk_02_ids_present_count`: Must equal exactly 1,196.
   - `chunk_02_ids_missing_count`: Must equal exactly 0.
   - `unexpected_affidavits_count`: Any records in `public.candidate_affidavits` that do not belong to Chunk 02 must equal 0.
3. **State Partition Verification:** Confirms all 8 authorized states with exact expected sub-counts (AP: 197, AR: 55, AS: 126, BR: 267, CG: 92, DL: 228, GA: 36, GJ: 195) and asserts 0 records from foreign states.
