# KSHETRA — India's Political Intelligence Platform

---

## The Opportunity

India's **delimitation** — the first redrawing of constituency boundaries in 50+ years — is imminent. Alongside, **General Elections 2028/29** and continuous state elections create a massive, recurring demand for constituency-level political data. No product in India currently delivers this at scale to citizens, journalists, or media houses.

---

## The Product (Live & Working)

**Kshetra** is a mobile-first political intelligence app covering **all 31 states and 4,000+ assembly constituencies** with:

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| Interactive Constituency Map | Tap any constituency → see winner, party, margin, MLA details |
| MLA/MP Transparency | Assets, criminal records, education, wealth growth (from affidavits) |
| Delimitation Simulator | India's ONLY tool showing how seat redistribution will change your constituency |
| Election Analytics | Swing seats, anti-incumbency, party strength, cross-state comparison |
| Promise Tracker | Track government promises — delivered, pending, broken — with citizen verification |
| Political Timeline | Defections, by-elections, party splits — all tracked in real-time |
| Civic Dashboard | Local issues, sentiment, MLA responsiveness scoring |
| Multi-Language | English, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi (more planned) |

**Status**: Built, tested, working APK ready for Android. Not a concept — a product.
