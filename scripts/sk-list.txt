The [[Sikkim Legislative Assembly]] is the [[unicameral]] legislature of the state of [[Sikkim]], in [[Northeast India]]. The seat of the [[State legislative assemblies of India|Legislative Assembly]] is at [[Gangtok]],<ref name="cpa-sk">{{cite web | title=Parliament-Sikkim Legislative Assembly | publisher=[[Commonwealth Parliamentary Association]] | url=https://www.cpahq.org/directory/sikkim/ | language=en-GB | access-date=2 January 2026}}</ref> the capital of the state. The assembly sits for a term of five years, unless it is dissolved earlier.<ref name=constitution>{{Cite web|url=https://legislative.gov.in/sites/default/files/COI_1.pdf|title=The Constitution of India [As on 9th December, 2020]|website=[[Ministry of Law and Justice (India)#Legislative Department|Legislative Department]]|access-date=30 December 2023 |archive-date=26 November 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211126153125/https://legislative.gov.in/sites/default/files/COI_1.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>{{Rp|page=72}}<ref name="Tenure">{{cite web | title=Data, Charts and Analysis | website=The Mirrority | date=1 January 2009 | url=https://www.themirrority.com/data/sikkim-pradesh-assembly | access-date=30 January 2025 | archive-date=5 November 2024 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241105155842/https://www.themirrority.com/data/sikkim-pradesh-assembly | url-status=live }}</ref> Sikkim is the [[List of states and union territories of India by area|second-smallest state]] in India, covering {{Convert|7096|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}};<ref name="g484">{{cite web | title=TABLE 2.1- Area and population by States (Census-2011) | publisher=[[Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation]] | url=http://mospi.nic.in/mospi_new/upload/SYB2013/CH-2POPULATION/Table%202.1.xls | December 2025}}</ref> which accounts for 0.2 per cent of the total area of [[India]].<ref name="area">{{cite web | title=About Sikkim | website=Sikkim Tourism | url=https://sikkimtourism.gov.in/Public/ExperienceSikkim/history | language=rw | access-date=5 February 2025 | archive-date=24 August 2023 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230824195126/https://sikkimtourism.gov.in/Public/ExperienceSikkim/history | url-status=live }}</ref> The current population of Sikkim is 6.32 lakhs (630,000), making it the least populous state in India.<ref>{{cite web |title=Population and decadal change by residence : 2011 (Persons) |url=http://www.censusindia.gov.in/2011census/PCA/PCA_Highlights/pca_highlights_file/India/Chapter-1.pdf |publisher=Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India |access-date=2019-01-15 |archive-date=2016-06-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160624015117/http://www.censusindia.gov.in/2011census/PCA/PCA_Highlights/pca_highlights_file/India/Chapter-1.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>{{rp|p=2}}<ref name="Population">{{cite web | title=Government of Sikkim, India | website=Government of Sikkim, India | date=16 May 1975 | url=https://sikkim.gov.in/departments/sikkim-information-commission | access-date=30 January 2025 | archive-date=15 January 2025 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250115005522/https://sikkim.gov.in/departments/sikkim-information-commission | url-status=live }}</ref> Established in 1975, the Sikkim Legislative Assembly consists of 32 members [[direct elections|elected directly]]<ref name=constitution/>{{rp|at=Ch. III, Art. 170, Sect. 1}} from [[Single-member district|single-seat]] constituencies<ref name="cpa-sk"/> using the [[first-past-the-post]] system.<ref name="Establishment">{{cite web | title=Address To The Members Of The Sikkim Legislative Assembly, Gangtok | website=President of India | url=https://presidentofindia.nic.in/dr-apj-abdul-kalam/speeches/address-members-sikkim-legislative-assembly-gangtok | access-date=30 January 2025}}</ref>

Since the independence of India, the [[Scheduled Tribes]] (ST) and the [[Scheduled Castes]] (SC) and have been given [[reservation in India|reservation status]], guaranteeing political representation, and the Constitution lays down the general principles of [[positive discrimination]] for SCs and STs.<ref name=constitution>{{Cite web|url=https://legislative.gov.in/sites/default/files/COI_1.pdf|title=The Constitution of India [As on 9th December, 2020]|website=[[Ministry of Law and Justice (India)#Legislative Department|Legislative Department]]|access-date=30 December 2023 |archive-date=26 November 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211126153125/https://legislative.gov.in/sites/default/files/COI_1.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>{{Rp|pages=35,137}}<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/karnataka/2020/feb/17/reserved-uncertainty-or-deserved-certainty-reservation-debate-back-in-mysuru-2104413.html|title=Reserved uncertainty or deserved certainty? Twelve constituencies are reserved for the Scheduled Tribes.<ref name="ST Seats">{{cite web | title=Allocation of Seats and Delimitation of Constituencies | url=https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1663/5/a1950-43.pdf | access-date=20 March 2025}}</ref><ref name="Bhutia-Lepcha">{{cite web | title=Sikkim CM will convert seats reserved for Bhutia-Lepcha community to SC if re-elected: Chamling | website=[[The Hindu]] | date=13 April 2024 | url=https://www.thehindu.com/elections/sikkim-assembly/sikkim-cm-will-convert-seats-reserved-for-bhutia-lepcha-community-to-sc-if-re-elected-chamling/article68060941.ece | access-date=30 January 2025 | archive-date=8 December 2024 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241208194116/https://www.thehindu.com/elections/sikkim-assembly/sikkim-cm-will-convert-seats-reserved-for-bhutia-lepcha-community-to-sc-if-re-elected-chamling/article68060941.ece | url-status=live }}</ref> Among these, reservations are granted only to members of the [[Bhutia-Lepcha]] (BL) community. Despite being recognized as Scheduled Tribes in 2002, no seats have been reserved for the Limboo and Tamang communities in the Sikkim Legislative Assembly. == History ==

The [[State Council (Sikkim)|State Council]] was the legislature of the [[Kingdom of Sikkim]] until its merger with India in 1975. The members of the Council were deemed to be the new Legislative Assembly of the newly formed state.
