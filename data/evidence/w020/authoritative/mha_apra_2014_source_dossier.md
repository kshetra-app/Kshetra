# AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE DOSSIER: ANDHRA PRADESH REORGANISATION ACT, 2014

**Dossier Identifier:** `DOSSIER-W020-MHA-APRA-2014`  
**Source ID:** `MHA-APRA-2014`  
**Statutory Date:** `2014-03-01` (Assent of the President)  
**Appointed Day:** `2014-06-02`  
**Statutory Authority:** Parliament of India / Constitution of India (Articles 2, 3, and 4)  
**Act Reference:** Act No. 6 of 2014, published in The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1  
**Governing Sections & Schedules:** Section 15, Section 26, Schedule II, Schedule XXXI  
**Primary Evidentiary Scope:** Successor-state territorial division; statutory allocation of 119 Assembly Constituencies (19 SC, 12 ST, 88 General) to Telangana under Schedule XXXI; retention of 175 Assembly Constituencies (29 SC, 19 ST, 127 General) in residuary Andhra Pradesh under Schedule II.  

---

## 1. Statutory Provisions

1. **Section 15 (Allocation of Sitting Members):**
   - Allocates the existing 119 Assembly Constituencies situated in the territories specified in subsection (1) of Section 3 to the Legislative Assembly of the State of Telangana.
   - Enacts **Schedule XXXI** (State of Telangana) comprising Assembly Constituencies 1 through 119.
2. **Section 26 (Representation in Legislative Assemblies):**
   - Directs that the total number of seats in the Legislative Assembly of Telangana shall be 119.
   - Out of 119 seats:
     * Scheduled Castes (SC): **19** seats.
     * Scheduled Tribes (ST): **12** seats.
     * General: **88** seats.
3. **Appointed Day:**
   - 2 June 2014 (S.O. 655(E), dated 4 March 2014) when the State of Telangana formally came into existence.

---

## 2. Established Legal Facts

- **Constituency Extents:** Schedule XXXI directly incorporates the territorial descriptions from the Delimitation Order 2008 for ACs 1 through 119 without boundary alteration.
- **Seat Allocation Authority:** Establishes the governing statutory baseline for Telangana Legislative Assembly representation:
  $$\text{Total Seats} = 119, \quad \text{SC Seats} = 19, \quad \text{ST Seats} = 12, \quad \text{General Seats} = 88$$
- **Successor State Division:** Formally severs the composite State of Andhra Pradesh into two distinct constitutional entities.

---

## 3. Strict Negative Boundaries & Prohibited Over-Claims

- **Does NOT Establish 2015 Territorial Transfers:** APRA 2014 predates the 2015 Polavaram project administrative transfers enacted under G.S.R. 311(E). Section 3 defines the initial boundaries of Telangana as 10 named revenue districts.
- **Does NOT Mandate Algorithm Applied:** APRA 2014 does not specify or mandate the Hamilton/Largest Remainder algorithm. The numbers 19 SC and 12 ST were established by Parliament based on the frozen Census 2001 representation.
