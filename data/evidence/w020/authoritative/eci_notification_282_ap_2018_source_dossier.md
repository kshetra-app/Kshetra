## What the PDF establishes

The notification expressly amends **Table A of Schedule III** of the 2008 Delimitation Order for Andhra Pradesh, concerning:
- AC 53 — Rampachodavaram (ST)
- AC 67 — Polavaram (ST)

The changes implement territorial transfers arising from the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2015. This is an Andhra Pradesh territorial-extent amendment; it is not a national geometry source and not a Telangana polygon correction.

## Distinctions that must remain explicit

1. The 2008 ECI consolidated Order's original Table of Contents identifies Schedule III as composite Andhra Pradesh. Schedule II is the table of Legislative Assembly seat counts, not Andhra Pradesh territorial extents.
2. The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014, Section 15 and Second Schedule substitute Schedule III with 175 AP AC extents and insert Schedule XXVII — Telangana, Table A, with 119 Telangana AC extents. The prior internal dossier that asserted Schedule XXXI = Telangana is invalid.
3. The Lok Sabha proceedings PDF dated 19 December 2018 corroborates a Gazette of India reference O.N.55(E) dated 22 September 2018. The parliamentary proceedings are corroboration only; they are not the Gazette issue.
4. **The actual Gazette of India O.N.55(E) issue PDF and its exact Part/Section/issue/page metadata have not been acquired.**
5. **The Andhra Pradesh State Gazette counterpart or certified copy has not been acquired.** The notification's publication reference must not be confused with the retained ECI notification PDF.

## Legal acceptance

The ECI notification PDF is a primary notification artifact, but it does not alone close the legal-chain requirement or prove boundary geometry. A separate Gazette-issue copy/certified issue, Andhra Pradesh Gazette counterpart, verified 2015 transfer instrument and effective-date adjudication are still required.
