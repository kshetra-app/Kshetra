## 2. Verified scope of the ECI notification

The notification expressly amends **Table A of Schedule III** of the Delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies Order, 2008 for Andhra Pradesh, concerning:
- AC 53 — Rampachodavaram (ST).
- AC 67 — Polavaram (ST).

The changes implement territorial transfers arising from the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2015. This is an Andhra Pradesh territorial-extent amendment; it is not a national geometry source and not a Telangana polygon correction.

## 3. Correct schedule and version distinctions

- The original 2008 ECI Order PDF's own Table of Contents identifies Schedule II as the assembly-seat-count table and Schedule III as composite Andhra Pradesh. It identifies original Schedule XXXI as Delhi.
- The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014, Section 15 and Second Schedule substitute Schedule III with successor-State Andhra Pradesh's 175 AC extents and insert Schedule XXVII — Telangana, Table A, with 119 AC extents. This amendment text should be preserved as a separate legal overlay; do not infer later renumbering without an applicable consolidated text.
- The prior text of this dossier that called the Andhra Pradesh schedule “Schedule II”, referred to Table B for the AC extents, and called Schedule XXXI Telangana is invalidated.
