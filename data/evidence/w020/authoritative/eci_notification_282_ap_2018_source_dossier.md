**Statutory Date:** `2018-09-22`  
**Publication Date:** `2018-09-24`  
**Statutory Authority:** Election Commission of India (Section 9(1)(b), Delimitation Act, 2002 read with Sections 15 & 26, Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014)  
**Notification Reference:** Commission's Notification No. 282/AP/2018(DEL), published in The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (iii)  
**Governing Table:** Table B (State of Andhra Pradesh, Schedule II)  
**Primary Evidentiary Scope:** Formal amendment of the territorial extents of two specified **Andhra Pradesh** Assembly Constituencies under Schedule II: **53-Rampachodavaram (ST)** (East Godavari District) and **67-Polavaram (ST)** (West Godavari District), incorporating the revenue mandals and villages transferred to Andhra Pradesh under G.S.R. 311(E).  

---

## 1. Statutory Provisions & Amendments

Pursuant to Section 9(1)(b) of the Delimitation Act, 2002, the Election Commission of India amended Schedule II of the Delimitation Order 2008 to reflect territorial changes arising from the Polavaram irrigation project transfers.

In Table B of the notification:
1. **53-Rampachodavaram (ST) (East Godavari District):**
   - Extent amended to include Chintur, Kunavaram, and Vararamachandrapuram mandals transferred from Khammam.
2. **67-Polavaram (ST) (West Godavari District):**
   - Extent amended to include Kukunoor and Velairpadu mandals, and the specified transferred villages of Burgampahad and Bhadrachalam mandals.

---

## 2. Established Legal Facts

- **Jurisdiction of Amendments:** The amendments enacted by Notification No. 282/AP/2018(DEL) apply **exclusively to the State of Andhra Pradesh (Schedule II)**.
- **Constituency Extents Updated:** Formally documents the updated boundaries of AC 53 and AC 67 in Andhra Pradesh.
- **Timing and Enactment:** Signed by the Election Commission on 22 September 2018 and published in the Gazette of India on 24 September 2018.

---

## 3. Strict Negative Boundaries & Prohibited Over-Claims

- **Does NOT Update Telangana Schedule XXXI (`MAP-06`):** The Notification contains zero amendments to Schedule XXXI (State of Telangana). It did **NOT** amend, dissolve, or reconstitute Telangana Assembly Constituencies **110 (Pinapaka)**, **118 (Aswaraopeta)**, or **119 (Bhadrachalam)**.
- **Does NOT Create Predecessor/Successor Versions for Telangana:** The continuing existence of ACs 110, 118, 119 in Telangana under their existing numbers is NOT proof of a new version.
- **Lineage Remains UNKNOWN (`MAP-07`):** Because no gazetted order enacted successor constituency versions for Telangana ACs 110, 118, 119 following the territorial transfer, any constituency lineage relationship remains strictly **`UNKNOWN`**.
- **No Mapping Rows in `public.constituency_mapping` (`MAP-02`):** `public.constituency_mapping` MUST NOT be populated with speculative predecessor/successor entries for Telangana constituencies.
