**Primary Evidentiary Scope:** Statutory territorial transfer of specified mandals/villages under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2015, transferring 7 revenue mandals/villages in Khammam District (State of Telangana) to East Godavari and West Godavari Districts (State of Andhra Pradesh) for the Polavaram Irrigation Project.  

---

## 1. Statutory Scope & Legal Transfer

Under Section 108(3) of the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014, the President promulgated the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2015 on 23 April 2015, coming into force at once.

The Order effected a statutory territorial transfer of specified mandals/villages under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2015:
1. **From Khammam District (Telangana) to East Godavari District (Andhra Pradesh):**
   - Kukunoor mandal (entire)
   - Velairpadu mandal (entire)
   - Burgampahad mandal (specified 6 revenue villages)
   - Chintur mandal (entire)
   - Kunavaram mandal (entire)
   - Vararamachandrapuram mandal (entire)
2. **From Khammam District (Telangana) to West Godavari District (Andhra Pradesh):**
   - Bhadrachalam mandal (specified revenue villages, excluding the revenue village of Bhadrachalam temple town)

---

## 2. Established Legal Facts

- **Nature of Transfer:** The transfer enacted by G.S.R. 311(E) is strictly a **statutory territorial transfer of specified mandals/villages under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2015**.
- **Revenue Administration Scope:** The transfer altered revenue district boundaries, revenue mandal jurisdictions, and police administration between Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
- **Telangana Baseline Invariant:** The total number of Assembly seats allocated to Telangana remained unchanged at 119.

---

## 3. Strict Negative Boundaries & Prohibited Over-Claims

- **Territorial Transfer != Constituency Lineage (`MAP-01`):** A statutory territorial transfer of specified mandals/villages under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2015 does **NOT** dissolve, reconstitute, or create new versions of Assembly Constituencies.
- **Zero Predecessor/Successor Relationship (`MAP-02`):** G.S.R. 311(E) does **NOT** establish a predecessor/successor relationship for Telangana ACs 110 (Pinapaka), 118 (Aswaraopeta), or 119 (Bhadrachalam).
- **Does NOT Update Schedule XXXI:** The Order does not amend the constituency extent definitions in Schedule XXXI of the 2014 Act or Delimitation Order 2008.
- **No Population Split Data:** The Order does not provide demographic cross-tabulations from Census 2011 for sub-mandal village fragments.
