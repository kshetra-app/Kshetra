---

## 3. Strict Negative Boundaries & Prohibited Over-Claims

- **Does NOT Establish Statutory Seat Allocations:** Census 2011 PCA population figures do **NOT** determine the current statutory reservation of the Telangana Legislative Assembly (which remains 19 SC and 12 ST under APRA 2014, based on frozen Census 2001 representation under the 84th Constitutional Amendment).
- **Does NOT Provide Pre-computed Sub-mandal Split Data:** The PCA does not cross-tabulate population for post-2011 sub-mandal territorial transfers without full revenue village registers.
- **Does NOT Legislate Delimitation:** Population data alone has no self-executing delimitation effect; boundaries and reservations require legislative enactment or Delimitation Commission orders.
