STATUTORY OBSERVATIONS:
1. AC 1 (Sirpur): Contains 100% of Sirpur (T), Kagaznagar, Dahegaon Mandals.
2. AC 2 (Chennur SC): Contains 100% of Chennur and Kotapalli Mandals.
   Kotapalli is FULLY inside AC 2 under the 2008 Delimitation Order.
3. AC 3 (Bellampalli SC): Contains Kasipet, Tandur, Bellampalli, Bhimini,
   Nennal, and Vemanpalli Mandals.
   Hajipur is NOT part of AC 3.
4. AC 4 (Mancherial): Contains strictly Luxettipet, Mancherial, and Dandepalli.
   Kotapalli is NOT part of AC 4.
5. AC 5 (Asifabad ST): Contains 100% of Asifabad and Tiryani Mandals.
6. Hajipur Mandal did NOT exist in 2008. Its constituent territory was carved
   out of Mancherial Mandal in October 2016. Because Mancherial Mandal was
   wholly in AC 4, Hajipur's containment in AC 4 is a DERIVED SUCCESSOR
   relationship under W014 temporal lineage.
