PARTICULARS OF G.O.Ms.No. 222 (MANCHERIAL DISTRICT):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Erstwhile District: Adilabad (District Code 533)
2. Newly Formed District: Mancherial District (LGD District Code 684)
3. Revenue Divisions: Mancherial and Bellampalli
4. Mandals Formed / Reorganised (18 total):
   Chennur, Luxettipet, Mancherial, Dandepally, Bellampalli, Kotapalli,
   Hajipur, Jaipur, Mandamarri, Kasipet, Tandur, Bhimini, Nennal,
   Vemanpalli, Jannaram, Bheemaram, Kannepally, Naspur.

5. Lineage Particulars for Hajipur Mandal:
   - Formation Type: Carved out as a new Mandal on 11.10.2016.
   - Predecessor Entity: Mancherial Mandal (constituent revenue villages
     bifurcated to form the newly created Hajipur Tahsil/Mandal office).
   - Predecessor Canonical ID: TS-MDL-5321 (Mancherial)
   - Successor Canonical ID: TS-MDL-5329 (Hajipur)
   - Transition Type: 'split'
   - Effective Date: 2016-10-11
   - Statutory Citation: G.O.Ms.No. 222, Revenue (DA-CMRF) Dept, dated 11.10.2016
   - Electoral Delimitation Impact: Article 170 boundary freeze preserves 2008
     Assembly Constituency boundaries. Constituent villages remain part of
     AC 4 (Mancherial).

4. Mandals Formed / Reorganised (15 total):
   Sirpur (T), Kagaznagar, Dahegaon, Tiryani, Asifabad, Bejjur, Chintalamanepalli,
   Jainoor, Kerameri, Koutala, Lingapur, Penchikalpet, Rebbena, Sirpur (U), Wankidi.
