- **Exact Title:** Order No. 46 — Delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies in the State of Andhra Pradesh
- **Issuing Authority:** Delimitation Commission of India (Nirvachan Sadan, Ashoka Road, New Delhi)
- **Bench / Signatories:**
  - Justice Kuldip Singh (Retired Judge, Supreme Court of India) — Chairperson

  - M. Narayana Rao (State Election Commissioner, Andhra Pradesh) — Member ex-officio
- **Statutory Authority:** Sub-section (1) of Section 10 read with Sub-section (2) of Section 9 and Sub-section (2) of Section 4 of the Delimitation Act, 2002 (Act No. 33 of 2002), as amended by the Delimitation (Amendment) Act, 2003

## 2. Instrument 2: Presidential Order Promulgating Delimitation Order 2008 (S.O. 382(E))

- **Exact Title:** Delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies Order, 2008
- **Issuing Authority:** President of India (Ministry of Law and Justice, Legislative Department, Government of India)
- **Signatory:** Promulgated by the President of India (Pratibha Devisingh Patil)
- **Notification Identifier:** **S.O. 382(E)**
- **Publication Date:** `2008-02-19`
- **Effective / Commencement Date:** `2008-02-19` (Paragraph 2: "This Order shall come into force at once", with deferred date 2008-03-20 applicable exclusively to Meghalaya and Tripura)
- **Gazette Publication:** The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), No. 250, New Delhi, Tuesday, February 19, 2008 / Magha 30, 1929 (Saka)
- **Operative Legal Status:** **`ENACTED_COMMENCED`**
- **Statutory Authority:** Sub-section (2) of Section 10 of the Delimitation Act, 2002 (33 of 2002) read with Articles 82 and 170(3) of the Constitution of India and Section 8(1) of the Representation of the People Act, 1950 (43 of 1950)
- **Governing Schedule for Andhra Pradesh:** **Schedule III** (State of Andhra Pradesh) in the original 2008 ECI Order; in 2014, Section 15 and the Act's Second Schedule substitute Schedule III with the successor-State Andhra Pradesh schedule.
  - Original Schedule III, Table A: Assembly Constituency territorial extents (294 composite-AP ACs; 48 SC, 19 ST, 227 general as the historic seat-allocation split).
  - Original Schedule III, Table B: Parliamentary Constituency territorial extents (42 composite-AP PCs).
- **Subsequent Statutory Succession:**
  - Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 (Act No. 6 of 2014), Section 15 and its Second Schedule substitute Schedule III with the 175-AC Andhra Pradesh schedule and insert **Schedule XXVII — Telangana**, Table A, with 119 AC extents. Do not label Telangana as Schedule XXXI; the original 2008 ECI PDF's Table of Contents identifies original Schedule XXXI as Delhi.

## 4. Key Statutory Passages

### Passage 1: Delimitation Act, 2002 — Section 10(2)
> *"Every order made by the Commission under section 9 shall be published in the Gazette of India and in the Official Gazettes of the States concerned and shall have the force of law and shall not be called in question in any court. ... Every such order shall come into force on such date as may be specified by the President by notification in the Gazette of India."*

### Passage 2: Presidential Order S.O. 382(E) — Paragraph 2
> *"2. The orders of the Delimitation Commission issued under sub-section (1) of section 10 of the Delimitation Act, 2002 (33 of 2002) in respect of delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly constituencies in all the States and Union Territories except the State of Meghalaya and Tripura shall come into force at once."* (Published 19 February 2008).

### Passage 3: Delimitation Order 2008, original Schedule III, Table A (Andhra Pradesh)
> *"Total Number of Seats: 294; Number of Seats reserved for the Scheduled Castes: 48; Number of Seats reserved for the Scheduled Tribes: 19."*
