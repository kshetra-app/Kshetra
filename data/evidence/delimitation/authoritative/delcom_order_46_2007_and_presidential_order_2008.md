## 1. Keep the legal instruments distinct

The repository previously conflated three different things:
1. Delimitation Commission of India Order No. 46 for Andhra Pradesh, dated 31 May 2007 (the repository dossier records Notification No. 282/AP/2007-III; its original Gazette issue/PDF is not acquired in the evidence package inspected here).
2. The Presidential notification S.O.382(E), dated 19 February 2008. A later official Gazette of India PDF, archived from its exact official URL, recites that the Delimitation Order, 2008 was published vide S.O.382(E), dated 19 February 2008. This confirms the publication reference/date through a later official recital, not the complete original 2008 issue metadata.
3. The Election Commission of India’s consolidated Delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies Order, 2008 PDF containing the schedules and extents.

The identifier 282/AP/2007-III recorded for the 2007 Commission order must not be relabelled as the 2008 Presidential notification. The 2008 publication reference supported by the later official Gazette recital is S.O.382(E). The prior inventory references to S.O.383(E) and S.O.453(E) as the publication number for this order are unsupported by the evidence reviewed and have been removed from the corrected inventory.

The present 4,123 AC registry reflects a later legal period, including the post-2022 90-seat J&K allocation, and must not be cited as a direct total from the 2008 order.

## 4. Successor states and the 2018 notification

The 2008 order predates creation of the separate State of Telangana. It contains no schedule named Telangana. Its Schedule III provides Assembly and Parliamentary extents for the then-composite Andhra Pradesh. The division into 175 Andhra Pradesh and 119 Telangana Assembly Constituencies belongs to the later Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 and relevant follow-on legal instruments; it is not accurate to say that Section 15 of that Act re-enacted the 2008 order’s Schedule XXXI as Telangana.

The independently hash-verified ECI Notification No. 282/AP/2018(DEL) PDF in master (102,133 bytes; SHA-256 13201bb665a5d29853862d0bc0c07175c319a61d5b39b2c838fd0ec3882737da) describes a boundary-extent amendment to Schedule III, Table A for Andhra Pradesh, limited to 53-Rampachodavaram and 67-Polavaram. It is not an amendment establishing a Telangana schedule and is not the Gazette issue itself.
