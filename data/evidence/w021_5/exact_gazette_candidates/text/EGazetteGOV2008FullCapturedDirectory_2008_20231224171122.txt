 O.N.62(E).-WHEREAS, in pursuance of clause (d) of sub-section (2) of Section 9 read

     with sub-section (2) of Section 4 of the Delimitation Act, 2002(33 of 2002), as

    amended by the Delimitation (Amendment) Act, 2003 (3 of 2004), the

     Delimitation Commission has passed its final order (Order No. 30) determining

     the extent of Assembly and Parliamentary Constituencies in the State of NCT of

    DELHI on 14th September , 2006; and

       WHEREAS, after publication of final order, the Chief Electoral Officer,

   NCT of Delhi, vide her letters no. CEO/EL.G/102(3)/2008 dated 19th May, 2008.

    and 22nd May, 2008 intimated to the Commission that various District Election

     Officers have pointed out some anomalies in respect of geographical congruity in

     the newly delimited Assembly Constituencies under their charge; and

       WHEREAS, the Delimitation Commission setup a committee to undertake

   an on - the - spot verification of the areas concerned to verify the reported

    formation of islands in ACs of NCT of Delhi; and

        WHEREAS, the said Committee of Delimitation Commission, after on - the

      - spot verification, found that in many instances some EBs are not contiguous

     with the other areas of the constituencies ;n which they have been included as

     per the Commission's order and form islands in adjoining constituencies; and

     WHEREAS, the above said Committee of Delimitation Commission reported

     that these discrepancies have occurred because of haphazard numbering of

    Enumeration Blocks of a particular colony/locality by the office of the Registrar

     General of India and that the maps provided by RGI office were also not legible

    and location of these EBs on the maps has also not been properly indicated as

     revealed by the on - the - spot verification; and
       WHEREAS, the Commission has considered and verified the facts

     brought to its notice by the Chief Electoral Officer, NCT of Delhi in the light of

     geographical contiguity and the provisions of the Delimitation Act, 2002 and
    many of the facts brought to the notice of the Commission by the Chief Electoral

       Officer, NCT of Delhi have been found to be correct.

        NOW, THEREFORE, by way of this corrigendum to correct the above

      errors arising from inadvertent slips and having regard to the provisions of the

      Delimitation Act, the Delimitation Commission hereby orders that -

             In Table A of the Commission's Notification No.282/DL/2006-Vol.V, dated

     20th September, 2006 :-
     (1) EB No. 107 of DMC Ward No. 28 shall be excluded from the extent of 16 -

           Trinagar AC and included in 17 - Wazirpur AC.
   (2) EB No. 179 of DMC Ward No. 34 shall be excluded from the extent of 5 -

            Badli AC and included in 6 - Rithala AC.

   (3) EB No. 434 of DMC Ward No. 33 shall be excluded from the extent of 6 -

            Rithala AC and included in 13 - Rohini AC.

   (4) EBs No. 199 to 200 of DMC Ward No. 83 shall be excluded from the

          extent of 68 - Gokulpur (SC) AC and included in 63 - Seemapuri (SC)

          AC.

   (5) EB No. 110 of DMC Ward No. 84 shall be excluded from the extent of 62 -

         Shahdara AC and included in 63 - Seemapuri (SC) AC.

   (6) EB No. 152 of DMC Ward No. 85 shall be excluded from the extent of 63 -

         Seemapuri (SC) AC and included in 68 - Gokulpur (SC) AC.

   (7) EBs No. 52 to 54 of DMC Ward No. 65 shall be excluded from the extent

            of 53 - Badarpur AC and included in 54 - Okhla AC.

        The Commission's order No. 30, dated 20th September, 2006 stands

   amended accordingly.
