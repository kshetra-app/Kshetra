## 3. Successor-State amendment established by the 2014 Act

A government-hosted copy of the Gazette/Act PDF was downloaded and verified:
- Title: Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 (Act No. 6 of 2014)
- URL: https://apseeds.ap.gov.in/PDF/apreorg/APRegACT2014.pdf
- Bytes: 231,596; SHA-256: `84cd566b917f9a3f600c47f6540520c950166b424a964ad37d64dc3c968114aa`
- PDF: 71 pages; PDF identity with the India Code-hosted copy has not yet been compared.

The Act's own text states:
- Section 15(1): on the appointed day, the 2008 Order stands amended as directed in the Act's Second Schedule.
- The Second Schedule substitutes Schedule III with a new “SCHEDULE — III / ANDHRA PRADESH / TABLE A — ASSEMBLY CONSTITUENCIES” containing Andhra Pradesh's 175 AC extents.
- After Schedule XXVI it inserts “SCHEDULE — XXVII / TELANGANA / TABLE A — ASSEMBLY CONSTITUENCIES” with 119 AC extents.
- The same Second Schedule updates successor-State seat allocations to 175 AP ACs and 119 Telangana ACs; it does not make the original unamended 2008 PDF's Schedule XXXI into Telangana.

Because the Act inserts a new Schedule XXVII between original Schedules XXVI and XXVII, do not infer the numbering of later schedules in the post-amendment consolidation unless supported by a later consolidated statutory text/version. Preserve the base-order numbering and amendment overlay separately. The 2018 ECI notification itself refers to Table A of Schedule III for Andhra Pradesh.
