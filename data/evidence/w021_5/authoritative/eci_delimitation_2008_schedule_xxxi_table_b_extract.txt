Reason: this file asserted that the 2008 Order's Schedule XXXI was re-enacted for Telangana under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014. The recovered and independently parsed 572-page ECI Order shows that Schedule XXXI is the DELHI schedule (pages 543–556), and the 2008 Order's Andhra Pradesh extents are under Schedule III (pages 8–32), Table A — Assembly Constituencies.

The rows previously shown here (for example Malkajgiri, Quthbullapur, Kukatpally and Hyderabad-area constituencies) appear to have been drawn from the historical composite Andhra Pradesh Schedule III, but must not be treated as a separate, current Telangana statutory schedule or mapped by their old composite-state serials to current Telangana AC numbers. The current 119-seat Telangana jurisdiction is governed by later legal instruments; the 2014 Reorganisation Act and subsequent orders must be reconciled independently.
