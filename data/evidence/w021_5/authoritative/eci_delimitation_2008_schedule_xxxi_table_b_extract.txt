INVALIDATED — DO NOT USE AS AUTHORITATIVE TELANGANA SCHEDULE
===========================================================

This legacy file is wrong as labelled. The claim “Schedule XXXI: Telangana” is not supported by the primary 2008 ECI Order PDF or the 2014 Act's amendment text.

Verified facts:
- Original, unamended ECI Delimitation Order, 2008: Schedule III = composite Andhra Pradesh (pages 8–32); Schedule XXVII = Tripura (pages 450–457); Schedule XXXI = Delhi (pages 543–556); Schedule XXXII = Puducherry (pages 557–560); Jammu and Kashmir Assembly constituencies appear in the Annexure.
- Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 (Act No. 6 of 2014), Section 15 and Second Schedule replace Schedule III with Andhra Pradesh territorial extents, and after Schedule XXVI insert a schedule explicitly titled “SCHEDULE — XXVII / TELANGANA / TABLE A — ASSEMBLY CONSTITUENCIES”.
- Do not conflate the original Order's schedule numbering with a fully consolidated post-2014 numbering. Any later schedule number for jurisdictions following the insertion must be cited to an actual applicable consolidated legal text and version, not inferred from this file.
- The 2018 ECI Notification No. 282/AP/2018(DEL) amends Table A of Schedule III (Andhra Pradesh) for 53-Rampachodavaram (ST) and 67-Polavaram (ST); it does not amend Telangana geometry.
- The original Gazette issue for S.O.382(E), the O.N.55(E) issue, and the Andhra Pradesh State Gazette counterpart remain OPEN.
