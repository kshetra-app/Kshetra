Reason: this file incorrectly labelled Andhra Pradesh Assembly Constituency extents as “Schedule II / Table B”. The hash-verified 572-page ECI Delimitation Order, 2008 archival copy shows:
- Schedule I (pages 4–5): Lok Sabha seat-allocation summary.
- Schedule II (pages 6–7): Legislative Assembly seat-allocation summary.
- Schedule III (pages 8–32): ANDHRA PRADESH (the composite, pre-2014 state).
- Schedule III, Table A: ASSEMBLY CONSTITUENCIES and territorial extents.
- Schedule III, Table B: PARLIAMENTARY CONSTITUENCIES.
- Schedule XXXI (pages 543–556): DELHI.
- Schedule XXXII (pages 557–560): PUDUCHERRY.
- Jammu and Kashmir Assembly Constituency extents appear in the Annexure (pages 561–571), not Schedule XII.
- The 2008 order has no separate Telangana schedule. The 2014 successor-state split into 175 Andhra Pradesh and 119 Telangana Assembly Constituencies is a later legal regime and must be traced through the Reorganisation Act and subsequent instruments.
