## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Kshetra is India's most comprehensive democracy dashboard — putting every assembly constituency, MLA, election result, civic issue, and government promise at your fingertips.

**🗺️ Interactive Election Map**
Explore 806+ constituencies across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, and Maharashtra with rich polygon maps. Color by party, margin, reservation, population, literacy, or turnout. Compare constituencies side by side.

**👤 MLA Profiles & Candidate X-Ray**
View detailed MLA profiles, election history, wealth declarations, criminal records, and red flags. Track defections and party-hopping in real-time.

**📊 Election Analytics**
Deep analytics dashboard with party strength analysis, swing seat identification, district heatmaps, and anti-incumbency metrics. Cross-state comparisons powered by real data.

**🏛️ Promise Tracker**
Hold governments accountable. Track election promises from "Promised" to "Delivered" or "Broken". View the Promise Delivery Index for each state.

**📢 Civic Issues Dashboard**
Report and track civic issues in your constituency. Upvote issues, follow progress, tag your MLA, and see constituency sentiment analysis.

**🔄 Delimitation Simulator**
Understand how upcoming delimitation will reshape India's electoral map. Run simulations, compare before/after seat allocations, and see how boundary changes affect your constituency.

**🎓 Leadership Academy**
Build civic knowledge through structured modules, earn badges, and participate in community challenges. Track your Civic Score.

**🌍 Multilingual**
Available in English, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, and Marathi with more languages coming.

**📱 Offline-First**
All constituency data works offline. Sync when connected. Fast, smooth, and data-efficient.

---
Built with verified Election Commission data. Open source. No ads. No tracking.
